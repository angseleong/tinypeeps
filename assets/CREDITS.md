## Notes

- The page asks not to resell unmodified copies or claim the assets as your own. We use them inside
  an app, not as a pack.
- The older "KayKit: Character Animations" 1.2 on OpenGameArt (also CC0) is **not** used: the itch.io
  1.1 pack above is the current one and has the `Rig_Medium` sets with sitting and waving.
- The Peeps are Kenney's Mini Characters, chosen by the maintainer over the KayKit Adventurers (no office
  clothes in any KayKit pack, free or paid). The Adventurers and the KayKit animations stay for the
  `office-fps` spike only.
- The Adventurers characters ship with no animations; the clips come from the animation pack and bind
  to the characters by bone name (same `Rig_Medium` skeleton).
- The free Furniture Bits has no desk with a monitor, server rack or coffee machine. Those are built
  from these pieces or modelled by us later, and listed here if added.
- Shading is ours (see [docs/office.md](../docs/office.md#art)).
